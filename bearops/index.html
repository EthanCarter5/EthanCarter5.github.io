BearOps

Baseball development from one iPhone on a tripod.

BearOps is an iPhone app for pitchers, players, parents and coaches. It is made by Bear Ops LLC in North Carolina and is currently in private beta.

What it does

  • Velocity & break: pitch speed, vertical and horizontal break, and pitch type, filmed from behind the pitcher. Speed is labeled beta while it is checked against radar.
  • Mechanics: the delivery tracked from the side, with landing, release and front-leg measurements compared with the athlete's own baseline.
  • Session reports: a summary after every session that athletes keep and can share with their coach.
  • Team tools: schedules, announcements, rosters and scouting for coaches and families.

Coach uploads

When an athlete's coach turns it on, finished sessions upload over Wi-Fi to a private folder in that coach's own Google Drive. BearOps can only see the files and folders it creates there.

Access

BearOps is invitation-only during the beta. Coaches invite athletes and families from inside the app.
