BearOps Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This policy covers the BearOps iPhone app and its services, run by Bear Ops LLC ("BearOps", "we"). BearOps is in private beta and is available only by invitation.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and the team, role and athlete you are linked to.
  • Team content: rosters, schedules, announcements, messages and reports that a team's coaches and members add.
  • Recordings and measurements: pitch and mechanics videos you record, the measurements made from them (such as speed, break and body-position data), and any radar readings you type in.
  • Device: a push notification token, the app version and the iPhone model, so we can deliver notifications and fix problems.

Where it is kept

  • On your iPhone: videos and measurements are recorded and measured on the phone.
  • Your coach's Google Drive: if your coach assigns you a tool, finished sessions (videos, measurements and the session report) upload over Wi-Fi to a private folder in that coach's Google Drive. BearOps's access to that Drive is limited to the files and folders BearOps creates.
  • BearOps servers (Google Cloud and Firebase): account sign-in, team membership and team content, and the upload records needed to resume and confirm uploads.
  • AI assistant: if you use Ask BearOps, your question and the team data needed to answer it are sent through our server to Google's Gemini service to produce the answer.

How we use it

Only to run BearOps: measure pitches and mechanics, build reports, share sessions with the coach you are linked to, send team notifications, and improve the accuracy of BearOps's measurements. We do not sell personal information and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

  • Coaches and the team owner see the team's athletes and the sessions shared with them.
  • Parents and athletes see only the athletes they are linked to.
  • Service providers that host BearOps (Google Cloud, Firebase, Apple push notifications) process data on our behalf.

Google user data

BearOps asks a team owner for access to their Google Drive only to store athlete sessions they receive. It uses the limited "files created by this app" permission, does not read other files, and does not transfer Google user data to anyone except as needed to provide this feature. BearOps's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Young athletes

For youth teams, accounts are managed by a parent or guardian, who controls what is recorded and shared for their athlete.

Your choices

  • Turn off uploads or delete sessions in the app at any time.
  • Ask your team owner, or BearOps, to remove your account and team data. We delete it within 30 days.
  • A team owner can disconnect BearOps from their Google account at any time in their Google Account settings.

Contact

Bear Ops LLC, North Carolina. Contact us through your team owner or the support email shown on BearOps's Google sign-in screen.

We will update this page if our practices change and change the effective date above.
